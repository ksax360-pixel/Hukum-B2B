# Vendor Onboarding & RFQ

## Vendor steps

1. Account (email, mobile, password, language, OTP)
2. Company (legal names, CR, VAT, address, type)
3. Business (industries, categories, markets, MOQ, lead time)
4. Documents (CR, VAT, certificates — configurable by country/industry)
5. Verification (draft → submitted → AI → review → verified)

## RFQ flow

Buyer requirement → AI understands → category/vendors suggested → buyer approves → RFQ distributed → quotations → comparison → communication → buyer decision

AI must not auto-select a vendor.

## Matching reasons (factual)

Supports category, quantity, serves location, has certification, matches specs, verified status. Internal score may exist but is not presented as a guarantee.
